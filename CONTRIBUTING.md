Thank you for your interest in the Tickoni creative universe.

This repository contains Tickoni lore, characters, stories, dialogue, illustrations, comics, release narratives, worldbuilding, and related creative material.

These contribution terms are intended to allow community discussion and suggestions without creating uncertainty about the ownership or future development of Tickoni.

For purposes of these terms, **Repository Owner** means Victor Genin, publishing under the name DeepRND.

The Repository Owner is under no obligation to:

* accept or incorporate a submission;
* respond to a submission;
* explain why a submission was rejected;
* preserve or publish a submission;
* provide attribution;
* provide payment, royalties, revenue participation, or other compensation; or
* obtain the contributor's approval for later Tickoni material.

A submission becoming part of Tickoni does not create employment, partnership, agency, joint ownership, or any other continuing relationship with the contributor.

## Creative Submissions

A **Submission** means material intentionally proposed, supplied, or suggested for possible use in Tickoni, including through:

* pull requests;
* issues;
* GitHub Discussions;
* comments and review comments;
* story or plot suggestions;
* character concepts or designs;
* names, dialogue, scenes, settings, themes, or worldbuilding;
* illustrations, sketches, comics, or visual concepts;
* release-story suggestions; and
* other creative ideas, feedback, or material offered for possible use by Tickoni.

Technical bug reports and ordinary discussion that do not intentionally propose creative material are not treated as creative Submissions merely because they appear in this repository.
